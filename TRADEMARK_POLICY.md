# Trademark policy

Pi TV is a working name; ownership and clearance of names, logos and marks have not been established. Do not imply endorsement by the project, Raspberry Pi or another party. Respect third-party trademark rights and identify the source of marks before use.

This draft does not grant trademark rights or restrict rights granted by copyright licenses. Commercial redistribution and private modifications are permitted under the applicable licenses; no upstream contribution is required. The initial Session 1C.4B review is recorded; mark ownership, permitted uses and final clearance remain open release gates. See [release gates](docs/engineering/RELEASE_GATES.md).

Third-party use must be checked against the current [Raspberry Pi trademark rules](https://www.raspberrypi.com/trademark-rules/) and [Apple trademark guidelines](https://www.apple.com/legal/intellectual-property/guidelinesfor3rdparties.html). Raspberry Pi’s published rules include acknowledgement and restrictions on branding and commercial uses; their applicability to any particular use requires review. Apple restricts use of its marks and logos. These references do not establish clearance or project ownership. Raspberry Pi is a trademark of Raspberry Pi Ltd.
