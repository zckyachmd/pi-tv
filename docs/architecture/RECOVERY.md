# First boot, enrollment and recovery

Owner direction: a prepared appliance, TV display and iPhone must suffice for onboarding, ordinary control, controller management and recoverable application faults, with BLE and without shared Wi-Fi or extra input peripherals. The former independent local HID confirmation proposal is superseded as a product requirement. The following security ceremonies are candidates, **not ratified or implementation authorization**; production is blocked until independent security review and physical tests.

## Candidate initial ownership

Durably unowned state may automatically display a short-lived, one-use QR containing a device identity anchor and high-entropy enrollment authorization. The phone authenticates the device to that anchor before sensitive material, binds authorization and identities to a reviewed mutual-authentication transcript, compares a candidate transcript-bound phrase displayed on both devices, and explicitly confirms on the phone. Phrase construction and protocol are unselected. Success requires atomic durable first-owner commit and QR consumption; concurrency, cancellation, expiry, uncertain commit, power loss and corrupted ownership state fail closed. Never treat corruption as unowned. QR visibility is not independent physical input or proof of distance; a photographed QR can enable a race. Shoulder surfing, relay, attempts, races and renewal require review. Automatic bounded renewal may only be considered for never-owned appliances under a reviewed policy.

## Owned device and recovery

An authenticated owner, not every controller, may authorize and explicitly approve adding a phone under reviewed policy. An owned device never automatically opens enrollment on disconnect, restart or radio loss. Revoke is persisted before success and terminates sessions. Reset and identity rotation require a separately reviewed authenticated owner or recovery action with explicit scope; preserve provider profiles unless explicitly authorized for erasure.

Candidate replacement recovery uses an independently backed-up high-entropy recovery credential and device identity anchor. The replacement phone authenticates the device and proves recovery authority without an existing controller or TV input; no unauthenticated request may disrupt playback or trigger destructive action. Atomic replacement, invalidation and rotation of recovery proof, loss/revocation policy, storage, backup and crash semantics remain security blockers. Loss of all controllers and recovery proof requires documented maintenance/re-provisioning, never an insecure bypass. Fatal OS, storage or radio faults may also need maintenance.

Prepared media or documented preparation on an existing OS is a distribution requirement; no assumption that iPhone flashes media. Automatic bounded process/display recovery must preserve trust and profiles and must not reboot or modify unrelated shared-host workloads. Test no-HID boot, wrong/stolen/replayed QR and recovery proof, racing enrollment, power loss at each durable boundary, owned-device restart, profile preservation, loss of proof, and BLE-only operation on physical devices.
