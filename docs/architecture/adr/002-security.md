# ADR 002 — Secure enrollment and sessions

Status: proposed, blocked; authority: product owner following independent security review.

**Context:** BLE advertisements and OS bonds do not establish application identity. **Alternatives:** TLS 1.3 over a bounded stream; a reviewed Noise pattern with suitable Rust and Swift libraries; unauthenticated or bespoke crypto rejected. **Direction:** mutually authenticated, encrypted replay-resistant channel pinned to QR identity with one-use enrollment and local confirmation; no suite selected. **Rationale/tradeoff:** durable revocable trust without network, at the cost of secure-channel integration and recovery complexity. **Evidence:** [rustls](https://docs.rs/rustls/latest/rustls/manual/_04_features/index.html), [SwiftNIO SSL](https://github.com/apple/swift-nio-ssl), [Snow](https://github.com/mcginty/snow), reviewed 2026-11-27; Snow states it lacks formal audit. **Consequences:** no production pairing until library and lifecycle are reviewed; never claim OS bonding alone suffices. **Validation:** interoperability, transcript binding, downgrade/replay, persistence crash and revocation tests, independent cryptographic review. See [threat model](../THREAT_MODEL.md).
