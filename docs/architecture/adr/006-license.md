# ADR 006 — License and marks

Status: owner decision on initial scope; provenance and distribution review pending.

The owner selected GPL-3.0-only for the first-party Rust core and TV launcher and a separate trademark policy. The iOS remote license is deferred, and iOS distribution is blocked pending licensing and platform compliance review. Design and visual assets require provenance review; documentation, build and packaging scripts, shared schemas and generated artifacts require explicit scope decisions. Third-party terms remain applicable. See [scope matrix](../../../LICENSING.md).

This decision does not establish rights in third-party or unverified contributions, authorize an iOS exception, establish trademark ownership, or approve production implementation. Session 1C.4B must assess provenance, dependency compatibility, corresponding source, current distribution terms and trademark rights. No noncommercial restriction or mandatory upstream contribution applies.
