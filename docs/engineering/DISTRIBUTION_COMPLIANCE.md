# Distribution compliance review

Status: release gates, not authorization to ship or a barrier to independent feasibility work. Determine the actual components, recipients and distribution method, and obtain qualified review before conveying artifacts. [Scope](../../LICENSING.md) and [provenance inventory](../planning/session-1c4-provenance.md) distinguish owner decisions from unresolved rights.

- **Debian packages:** For GPL-covered binaries, provide the GPL text and required notices and meet a permitted [GPLv3 section 6](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html) corresponding-source option for the actual distribution. Preserve component-specific notices and check each dependency's license and source obligations. Prepare an auditable source bundle and delivery process before release.
- **Prepared images:** Inventory the exact OS image, packages, firmware, fonts, codecs and assets; secure redistribution rights and applicable notices for each. GPL-covered works on an image require corresponding source under the applicable section 6 option. Avoid cloning device secrets. If a covered object is conveyed in or for a qualifying User Product under the conditions in section 6, supply applicable installation information needed to install and execute modified versions, subject to the license's stated exceptions. Assess the actual transaction and signing design; a package alone does not settle the image obligations.
- **Browser and DRM:** Determine the actual browser build, codecs, CDM and firmware, their provenance, redistribution permissions and terms. Do not bundle proprietary components merely because an installation can obtain them. No provider or DRM support is established.
- **iOS:** The remote's license is undecided; distribution of development builds, TestFlight and App Store builds remains blocked pending owner selection and review of rights, dependencies, signing, current terms and compatibility. The [Apple Developer Program License Agreement](https://developer.apple.com/support/terms/apple-developer-program-license-agreement/) distinguishes registered-device, TestFlight and App Store channels and imposes channel-specific requirements; external TestFlight requires Apple approval. Review the actual applicable agreement and [App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) before submission. No exception or compatibility conclusion is authorized here.

Before release, finalize third-party notices and an SBOM based on the actual artifact; determine exact corresponding source and installation information, delivery and retention; review trademark and provider rights; and obtain owner approval. GPL rights include commercial redistribution and do not require upstream pull requests.
